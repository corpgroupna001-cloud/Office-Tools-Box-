Team chat — rules

Do

  • Keep conversations professional and work-related.
  • Use chat to coordinate with your team and reach your admin.
  • Report any issue or misuse to your admin.

Don't

  • Don't share passwords, OTP codes, or bank/personal details in chat.
  • Don't post offensive, discriminatory, or harassing content.
  • Don't share confidential company data outside your team.

Calls are peer-to-peer and encrypted in transit; WorkSuite does not record them.
